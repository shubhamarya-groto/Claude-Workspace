## What the rule would not let me fix

Working only in whole sections is the right discipline, and it left five things standing:

1. **Both mobile heroes are still full-bleed orange** (`1238:2591`, `1238:2912`). White body copy on
   that orange is about 3:1 and fails AA, and the primary button is orange on orange so the main
   action all but disappears. **This is now the single worst defect in the set** and it cannot be
   swapped away until the kit has mobile sections.
2. **Every orange button still has a white label**, about 3:1. One theme setting fixes it across
   every page and every module at once. It is not a section problem.
3. **The footer is still one thin row** on all four pages. Elevate's footer is a global partial, not
   a section, so there is no kit component to swap in.
4. **The Home trust bar and hero fork cards** are unchanged. Neither is broken, both are plain.
5. **About mobile is still built to a different spec**, at 390 wide with its own band naming, and
   its hamburger is still an empty circle.

## What the swap introduced, and what is still kit filler

Worth knowing before this is shown:

- **Feature icons are empty tinted squares.** The kit ships them as placeholders. They read as
  unfinished at close range.
- **The FAQ answer line is kit filler**: "The answer sits under the question and stays short."
  Real answers exist for six of the seven questions and should be dropped in.
- **Image slots are grey boxes**, deliberately, including the three on the About story that are
  labelled for photos of Brian.
- The long real quotes clipped at the card edge when first placed. Fixed by letting them wrap.
