## What the product is

GoFindBuild (gofindbuild.com) is a two-sided **construction labour marketplace**.

- **Companies** post trade jobs and run a pipeline: applicants, interviews, hiring. Pitched as
  cheaper and faster than recruiters. Pricing is being reworked: per-applicant pricing is on the
  current site, Brian's new design proposes a flat monthly price (open, I-12).
- **Workers** are skilled-trade professionals and labourers. No resume or degree needed. They build
  one profile, get matched, and hear from employers.

The audience skews non-technical, blue-collar and mobile-first. Everything has to feel effortless
to someone who doesn't live in software, while looking trustworthy enough that a company pays.

**Visual direction:** "Middle ground", chosen 22 Jun: a clean neutral base with purposeful orange,
over Bright and Minimal. Most trustworthy for paying companies, easiest for workers, and it holds
up in data-heavy dashboards.
