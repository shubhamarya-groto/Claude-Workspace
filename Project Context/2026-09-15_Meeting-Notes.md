- Take Brian's current mockups to a proper, polished UI/UX direction.

Current page was identified as missing a problem-awareness section. Planned
order: Hero, Problem/pain-point, Solutions, supporting sections,
Testimonials/social proof, FAQs, Company and worker specific sections.
Goal is conversion, not just polish.

**3. Propose FAQs for both ICPs**
Companies and workers/job seekers. Team proposes, Brian reviews which questions
are actually relevant. Treated as refinable later since the copy is not locked.

**4. Plan for category-specific landing pages**
GTM expects pages targeting specific trades, plumbers being the example given.
These need: a logical place in the sitemap, discoverability, a possible home
under the relevant company/trade navigation, and the same design system as the
main site.

**5. Get the first Figma draft out quickly**
The most concrete near-term target. First draft of the page during that week
(week of 15 Sep), to get agreement on the Figma direction first. HubSpot
implementation to start mid/end of the following week, roughly a week allocated.
