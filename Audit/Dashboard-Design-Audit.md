# Dashboard Design Audit – consolidated (Final v2)

Employer dashboard UI/UX audit, desktop + responsive. Canonical source: `Source files/GoFindBuild_Design_Audit_Final_v2.xlsx` (latest complete version).

**39 issues** – 15 Critical, 16 Major, 8 Minor. IDs prefixed `R-` come from the responsive audit.

## Key findings (from the Summary tab)

- The absent top navigation bar is the single highest-leverage fix — it unblocks notifications, global search, account access, mobile responsiveness, and proper logout placement.
- 6 of 9 Critical issues are directly tied to the 'Add New Skill' modal flow — this component needs a full redesign before launch.
- The onboarding zero-state and notification absence are the two highest churn-risk issues for new employer signups.
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance failures (focus states, touch targets) may block enterprise and government client sales.
- Total: 26 issues across 5 screens. Recommend addressing all Critical issues before any marketing or growth spend.
